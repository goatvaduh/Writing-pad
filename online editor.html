Pen buttons and keys

For Wacom Express Keys and the touch ring, set each one to a keystroke in the Wacom driver, then click a field below and press that key. Scrolling over the canvas moves between pages, and holding Alt while you scroll changes brush size. To use the touch ring for brush size, set it to two keystrokes in the driver and bind them to Bigger brush and Smaller brush.

Pen side buttons

If a button does nothing, set it to Right Click (button 1) or Middle Click (button 2) in the Wacom driver.
